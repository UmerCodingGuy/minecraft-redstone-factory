Engineer Manual

How To Play

1. Place a Miner.

2. Connect Redstone power using a Lever, Torch or Button and Redstone Wire.

3. Place conveyor belts in front of machines.

4. Use Rotate before placing belts or machines.

5. Send resources through Crushers, Smelters and Crafters.

6. Finish the line with a Storage Chest.

7. Open Inventory and sell finished products.

Interact Tool: tap Levers and Buttons to activate them.

Splitter Belt: alternates items between two directions.

Multiple conveyor lines can enter the same belt, allowing simple belt merging.
